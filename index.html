System Summary

Dice

  • 2d8: base roll for all checks, add mods, compare to Challenge Score
  • Advantage: 3d8, summed
  • Disadvantage: 1d8
  • No stacking (min 1 die, max 3 dice, multiple sources cancel out)
  • Critical: two natural 8s among rolled dice
  • Fumble: two natural 1s
  • Both impossible on Disadvantage
  • d100: GM-called for random events
  • Gambit roll: GM-called for 50/50 events
  • Grace: builds on failed rolls, spend all at once to boost a roll, max 8, resets only on combat start/end

Attributes

  • Vigor: Health and endurance
  • Fortitude: Toughness and damage resistance
  • Strength: Physical power, lifting, and breaking
  • Dexterity: Precision, control, and agility
  • Intelligence: Knowledge, research, and learning
  • Arcane: Magic knowledge and manipulation
  • Charisma: Persuasion, presence, and social influence
  • Reflex: Quick reactions and evasive movement
  • Insight: Reading motives, lies, and hidden meaning
  • Awareness: Noticing surroundings, threats, and details

Creation

  • Species: applied first, up to +2 in any one stat, nets +3 overall (negatives allowed)
  • Pool: 12 points, +4 cap per stat, spent after species
  • Absolute Level 1 ceiling per stat: +6
  • Absolute max per stat: +8

Armor

  • Base Armor: 6
  • Formula: 6 + Fortitude, flat, no level scaling

Mana

  • Requires a spellcasting trait, not every character can cast
  • Formula: Level + Intelligence + Arcane
  • Costs: Cantrip 0, Tier 1 = 1, Tier 2 = 2, Tier 3 = 3, Tier 4 = 4, Tier 5 = 5
  • Tier unlocks: Level 1/3/5/7/9 (Tier 1 through 5)
  • Upcasting: any known spell can be cast at a higher tier for extra effect, up to Tier 6 max, even though no actual Tier 6 spells exist
  • Refill: half on short rest, full on long rest, potions (4-12 by tier), rare traits

Spellcasting

  • Governing stat: Arcane (both groups)
  • Sorceries: Arcane, Void, Abyss
  • Incantations: Elemental, Bestial, Divine, Blood
  • Locked into chosen school at creation, unless a trait says otherwise

Equipment

Weapon Attack = 2d8 + Atk + Accuracy Bonus (from spells and/or traits)

Weapon Damage = Weapon Base Damage + Damage Bonus (from traits and/or spells)

Weapon Crit = [Weapon Base Damage + Damage Bonus (from traits and/or spells)] * 2

Weight

Weight is calculated from equipped gear compared to max carry capacity and affects movement actions.

  • Light Load: less than 50% of max weight, +1 to any movement action
  • Medium Load: 50% to 75% of max weight, base movement
  • Heavy Load: above 75% up to 99% of max weight, -1 to any movement action
  • Overloaded: 100% or more of max weight, cannot move

Combat rules

Block, Dodge, and Parry can only be used against an incoming attack if you have an unused Action. Using one consumes that Action. No Action = no reactive defense.

  • Block: Defender contests with 2d8 + [Fortitude]. Win = reduce the damage by 50% (round down). Loss = you take full damage. Shield rule is configurable: auto-negate OR bonus to Block roll.
  • Dodge: Defender contests with 2d8 + Reflex. Win = negate all damage and reposition 1 tile (normal movement/collision rules apply; Overloaded cannot use this action). Loss = full damage, no reposition.
  • Parry: Requires an equipped item that is parry capable (weapon or shield). Defender contests with 2d8 + accuracy. Win = negate all damage and gain Riposte (Opportunity attack with +50% damage bonus rounded down melee range only). Loss = full damage and lose Reaction for the rest of the scene.
