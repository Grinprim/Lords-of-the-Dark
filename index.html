System Summary

Dice

  • 2d8: base roll for all checks, add mods, compare to Challenge Score
  • Advantage: 3d8, summed
  • Disadvantage: 1d8
  • No stacking (min 1 die, max 3 dice, multiple sources cancel out)
  • Critical: two natural 8s among rolled dice
  • Fumble: two natural 1s
  • Both impossible on Disadvantage
  • d100: GM-called for random events
  • Gambit roll: GM-called for 50/50 events
  • Grace: builds on failed rolls, spend all at once to boost a roll, max 8, resets only on combat start/end

Attributes

  • Vigor: Health and endurance
  • Fortitude: Toughness and damage resistance
  • Strength: Physical power, lifting, and breaking
  • Dexterity: Precision, control, and agility
  • Intelligence: Knowledge, research, and learning
  • Arcane: Magic knowledge and manipulation
  • Charisma: Persuasion, presence, and social influence
  • Reflex: Quick reactions and evasive movement
  • Insight: Reading motives, lies, and hidden meaning
  • Awareness: Noticing surroundings, threats, and details

Creation

  • Species: applied first, up to +2 in any one stat, nets +3 overall (negatives allowed)
  • Pool: 12 points, +4 cap per stat, spent after species
  • Absolute Level 1 ceiling per stat: +6
  • Absolute max per stat: +8

Armor

  • Base Armor: 6
  • Formula: 6 + Fortitude, flat, no level scaling

Mana

  • Requires a spellcasting trait, not every character can cast
  • Formula: Level + Intelligence + Arcane
  • Costs: Cantrip 0, Tier 1 = 1, Tier 2 = 2, Tier 3 = 3, Tier 4 = 4, Tier 5 = 5
  • Tier unlocks: Level 1/3/5/7/9 (Tier 1 through 5)
  • Upcasting: any known spell can be cast at a higher tier for extra effect, up to Tier 6 max, even though no actual Tier 6 spells exist
  • Refill: half on short rest, full on long rest, potions (4-12 by tier), rare traits

Spellcasting

  • Governing stat: Arcane (both groups)
  • Sorceries: Arcane, Void, Abyss
  • Incantations: Elemental, Bestial, Divine, Blood
  • Locked into chosen school at creation, unless a trait says otherwise

Equipment

Weapon Attack = 2d8 + Atk + Accuracy Bonus (from spells and/or traits)

Weapon Damage = Weapon Base Damage + Damage Bonus (from traits and/or spells)

Weapon Crit = [Weapon Base Damage + Damage Bonus (from traits and/or spells)] * 2

Weight

Weight is calculated from equipped gear compared to max carry capacity and affects movement actions.

  • Light Load: less than 50% of max weight, +1 to any movement action
  • Medium Load: 50% to 75% of max weight, base movement
  • Heavy Load: above 75% up to 99% of max weight, -1 to any movement action
  • Overloaded: 100% or more of max weight, cannot move

Combat rules

Block, Dodge, and Parry require an unused Action to use reactively against an incoming attack.

  • Block: 2d8 + Fortitude vs attack. Win = half damage. Loss = full damage. Shield: auto-negate or roll bonus (configurable).
  • Dodge: 2d8 + Reflex vs attack. Win = no damage, reposition 1 tile. Loss = full damage. Unavailable if Overloaded.
  • Parry: requires a parry-capable weapon/shield. 2d8 + accuracy vs attack. Win = no damage + Riposte (opportunity attack, +50% damage). Loss = full damage, lose Reaction for the scene.

Turn Structure & Action Economy

Turn Structure

  • Scene: one full cycle of every creature's turn. Ends when everyone has acted (Narrator may extend it).
  • Initiative: order of engagement + Narrator ruling. Unconscious creatures drop to the bottom of the order until revived.
  • NPC Initiative: uses a Turn Delay set in their stat block instead of rolled initiative.
  • Ambush: attacking an unaware target lets the attacker act before normal initiative/delay.
  • Opportunity Attack: when a creature leaves another's melee range, the remaining creature may make a melee attack against it (uses the Reaction).

Action Economy

  • Each Turn = 2 Actions. Only 1 Attack per turn by default (some traits bypass this).
  • Each Scene = 1 Reaction.
  • Held Action: hold 1 unused Action until your next turn, triggering it when a declared condition is met. (This is what powers Block/Dodge/Parry.)

Conditions

Buildup Mechanic

Poisoned, Toxic, Rotted, Bleed, Frost, and Madness use a Buildup meter. Weapons/spells/hazards add Buildup on hit (amount defined per source). Threshold = 20 + Fortitude (max 28 without other boosts). At Threshold, the condition procs and the meter resets to 0. If not topped up, Buildup decays by 2 per turn.

Action/Reaction-Denying Conditions

Conditions that remove Actions, Reactions, spellcasting, or trait use do not stack. Reapplying one only refreshes its duration.

  • Stunned: skip your next turn.
  • Silenced: cannot cast spells for 1 turn.
  • Hesitation: cannot make weapon or unarmed attacks for 1 turn.
  • Dazed: cannot use trait/skill abilities for 1 turn.
  • Frightened: no resistance (bypasses Buildup/checks entirely), freezes you in place. Cannot act or react for 1 turn unless the source states otherwise.

Other Conditions

  • Prone: movement 0, non-ranged attacks against you have Advantage, standing up costs 1 Action, cannot Dash.
  • Restrained: movement 0, attacks against you have Advantage, cannot attack. Escapes with a Strength check.
  • Slow / Haste: stacking, -1 or +1 tile movement per stack, expires next Scene if not reapplied.
  • Blinded: Disadvantage on your attacks, Advantage on attacks against you.
  • Vigil: Advantage on your attacks, Disadvantage on attacks against you.
  • Charmed: must follow the charmer's non-harmful commands, expires end of Scene if not reapplied.
  • Unconscious: Achieved when HP reaches 0, or when a trait/spell explicitly inflicts it. While Unconscious: counts as Prone, cannot act or use Reactions, cannot attempt Block/Dodge/Parry. Only Final Blows can harm you while Unconscious, normal attacks do nothing further. Ends immediately on receiving any healing that brings HP above 0, or when the Scene ends if nobody delivers a Final Blow (stabilizes at 1 HP). See Death & Hollowing below for what happens if a Final Blow lands.

Death & Hollowing

Limb Loss

If you lose a limb, you have to graft a diffrent one since healing itself will not regrow it. Through extensive healing processes, a grafted limb may eventually come to look and function like your original. True regrowth is possible, but extremely difficult to achieve.

Final Blow

Any creature adjacent to an Unconscious target may spend 1 Action to deliver a Final Blow instead of a normal attack. No roll required, it hits automatically.

Final Blow Resistance

Each character can resist 4 Final Blows before dying (Narrator rulings may adjust this number). Each Final Blow that connects reduces this by 1. At 0 remaining, death is triggered.

Undead Transformation

When death is triggered, before it becomes permanent, the character undergoes a special check (details withheld for now). Pass: the character survives, but gains the Undead condition and becomes Hollowed. Fail: permanent character death.

Hollowing Check

Once per in-game day, a Hollowed character rolls 2d8 (no modifier) against Challenge Score 10, escalating over time the longer they remain Hollowed. Fail: the character permanently loses their mind, this is character death. Pass: the character remains playable until the next day's check. A cure for Hollowing exists but is rare and impractical to pursue.

Rests

  • Rest (10+ min): rest at least 10 minutes to gain benefits, up to 4 hours. For each hour rested (including the first 10 minutes), regain Health equal to your Level. Regain Active trait uses.
  • Sleep (8 hrs): an extended rest, character does not act. Sleep 6+ hours: fully recover Health and Mana, or reduce Fatigue by 1 instead if you have any. Sleep 4+ hours: recover half Health and Mana. Sleep less than 4 hours: no recovery. Any sleep prevents Fatigue gain.
  • Fatigue: -2 to all rolls per stack, stacking. Level 6 Fatigue without sleeping is fatal.
