System Summary

Dice

  • 2d8: base roll for all checks, add mods, compare to Challenge Score
  • Advantage: 3d8, summed
  • Disadvantage: 1d8
  • No stacking (min 1 die, max 3 dice, multiple sources cancel out)
  • Critical: two natural 8s among rolled dice
  • Fumble: two natural 1s
  • Both impossible on Disadvantage
  • Gambit roll: GM-called, no mods, coin toss, player calls Heads or Tails, correct call = success, wrong call = fail
  • Grace: builds on failed rolls, spend all at once to boost a roll, max 6, resets only on combat start/end

Attributes

  • Vigor: Health, survivability
  • Fortitude: Resisting harm, armor
  • Strength: Lifting, breaking, raw power
  • Dexterity: Precision, agility
  • Intelligence: Research, learned knowledge
  • Arcane: Magical knowledge and manipulation
  • Charisma: Getting what you want by talking
  • Reflex: Dodging, reacting quickly
  • Insight: Reading intentions and hidden truths
  • Awareness: Perception of surroundings and danger

Creation

  • Species: applied first, up to +2 in any one stat, nets +3 overall (negatives allowed)
  • Pool: 12 points, +4 cap per stat, spent after species
  • Absolute Level 1 ceiling per stat: +6

HP

Total HP = Cumulative Base HP (table below) + (Vigor x Level)

Base HP grows every level regardless of Vigor investment. Vigor adds a bonus on top, scaling with Level.

Armor

  • Base Armor: 6
  • Formula: 6 + Fortitude, flat, no level scaling

Mana

  • Requires a spellcasting trait, not every character can cast
  • Formula: Level + Intelligence + Arcane
  • Costs: Cantrip 0, Tier 1 = 1, Tier 2 = 2, Tier 3 = 3, Tier 4 = 4, Tier 5 = 5
  • Tier unlocks: Level 1/3/5/7/9 (Tier 1 through 5)
  • Upcasting: any known spell can be cast at a higher tier for extra effect, up to Tier 6 max, even though no actual Tier 6 spells exist
  • Refill: half on short rest, full on long rest, potions (4-12 by tier), rare traits

Spellcasting

  • Governing stat: Arcane (both groups)
  • Sorceries: Arcane (generalist), Void (Gravity/Madness/Cosmic), Abyss (Summoning/Curses/Soul/Life Drain), forbidden
  • Incantations: Elemental (Fire/Lightning/Frost-Water/Nature/Earth), Bestial, Divine, Blood (Corruption), forbidden
  • Locked into chosen school at creation, unless a trait says otherwise

Equipment

Weapon Attack = 2d8 + Atk + Accuracy Bonus (from spells or traits)

Weapon Damage = Weapon Base Damage + Damage Bonus (from traits or spells)

Weapon

  • Requirements
  • Attack
  • Damage
  • Optional: skill, stat boost etc
